Latest Research Focus

Our laboratory strives to perform cutting-edge interdisciplinary research in an exciting and highly collaborative environment. We aim to uncover and characterize the 98.5% of the human genome that does not code for proteins, which we call non-coding RNAs (ncRNAs) (microRNA, piRNA, ceRNA, lncRNA). These molecules act as promising rheostats of gene regulation. We decrypt their roles in controlling development and diseases, especially in human cancers, by applying molecular biology techniques coupled with high-throughput omics approaches in cell lines and in vivo models.
